Gemma suggestions: review

For each clip, listen and compare. Red = the ASR word, green = Gemma's suggestion. Tick an edit only if the narrator really said Gemma's word. Leave it unticked to keep the ASR word. You can also type directly in the text box (the final text for the clip is whatever is in the box). Then press Accept text, Reject clip (bad audio, cut-off word) or Unsure. Clips you never decide keep the ASR text. Progress saves in this browser. Press Download every 10 clips.
